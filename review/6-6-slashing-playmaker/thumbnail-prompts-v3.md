## A — Posed Portrait + Social UI Stat Card

```
Bold, punchy YouTube-thumbnail composite — poster-grade, photoreal and
high-impact, NOT a muted cinematic movie still, 16:9 aspect ratio, single
unified frame — no split-screen, everything blends smoothly across the
same continuous shot.

TEXT: bold thumbnail text baked into the image. A dark frosted-glass card
occupies the left third of the frame with a gold border. At the top,
condensed white sans-serif with a gold outline reading exactly "FOUR-WAY
DEMIGOD" in large all caps. Below it, three horizontal stat rows, each
reading exactly one of: "90 PLAYMAKING — ELITE TIER", "88 FINISHING —
ELITE TIER", "84 SHOOTING — ELITE TIER", gold text on a thin dark bar. In
the card's bottom corner, a glowing gold circular badge reading exactly
"S-TIER". No other text, no watermark, no real brand or league names.

SUBJECT: a generated original character — 6'6" muscular point guard,
medium-brown skin, short fade haircut with a crisp line-up, light trimmed
beard, plain unbranded black jersey with crimson side panels and gold
trim, one bold gold number 4 on the chest and nothing else printed on it.
Confident hero pose, arms crossed, rendered LARGE and dominant, filling
~50% of the frame on the right side, chest-up, pushed to the foreground,
cleanly separated from the background. Expression: calm magnetic gaze,
relaxed brows, faintest composed half-smile — confident, never aggressive.
All facial features crisply sharp as the anchor of the shot.

KEY ELEMENTS: none beyond the card — the subject and the card are the
whole story, no clutter.

COMPOSITION: subject on a power third opposite the card, strong
subject-vs-background separation, camera at eye level, shallow depth with
a soft foreground light accent.

BACKGROUND: bold black-to-crimson gradient, blurred generic arena light
flares, no readable signage, no scoreboard text, no real team or league
logos anywhere.

LIGHTING: signature YouTube thumbnail lighting rig — a strong key light
sculpting the face with crisp highlights and controlled falloff, a soft
dream-light fill lifting the shadows with a subtle cinematic glow, and a
defined gold-and-crimson back light + hair light tracing a clean bright
rim along the hair, shoulders and silhouette, separating the subject
sharply from the background.

GRADE: vivid high-impact color grade, punchy high contrast, bright clean
exposure, rich saturated colors that pop off the screen, deep blacks and
bright highlights, crisp and glossy, poster-punchy, cohesive as one image.
16:9.
```

*Why it could perform:* highest information density (title + 3 real stats
+ grade, zero wasted space) and the easiest to template build-to-build —
already proven once with clean, correctly-spelled text via `nano_banana_pro`.

## B — Posed Action Shot + Amplified Reality

```
Bold, punchy YouTube-thumbnail composite — poster-grade, photoreal and
high-impact, 16:9 aspect ratio, single unified frame.

No text, no readable UI labels, no watermark.

SUBJECT: a generated original character — 6'6" muscular point guard,
medium-brown skin, short fade haircut with a crisp line-up, light trimmed
beard, plain unbranded black jersey with crimson side panels and gold
trim, one bold gold number 4 on the chest and nothing else printed on it.
Exploding above the rim for a powerful one-handed slam dunk, fierce
determined expression, rendered LARGE and dominant filling ~55% of the
frame, pushed to the foreground, camera at a low dramatic angle looking
up. All facial features crisply sharp as the anchor of the shot.

KEY ELEMENTS: a bright basketball mid-flight near his hand; ONE real
element of the shot amplified for impact — a subtle gold energy trail
streaking off his driving arm and the ball, like a heat-shimmer/motion-glow
effect, plausible rather than fantastical, reading as raw athleticism
rather than a superpower.

COMPOSITION: subject filling the frame on a power third, rim and net in
the lower foreground, strong separation from the background.

BACKGROUND: bold vivid crimson-to-black gradient color field, punchy high
contrast, soft vignette, blurred generic arena lights, no readable
signage or scoreboard text, no real team or league logos anywhere.

LIGHTING: signature YouTube thumbnail lighting rig — strong key light
sculpting the face, soft dream-light fill, a defined gold-and-crimson back
light + hair light tracing a bright rim along the silhouette.

GRADE: vivid high-impact color grade, punchy high contrast, bright clean
exposure, rich saturated colors, deep blacks and bright highlights, crisp
and glossy, poster-punchy. 16:9.
```

*Why it could perform:* pure kinetic energy, zero text-rendering risk
(nothing baked), and the "amplified" light trail gives it a premium-game
aesthetic without inventing a fantasy element that breaks the truthfulness
law — it's still recognizably an athletic dunk, just heightened.

concept without clutter.

COMPOSITION: subject centered-right on a power third, strong separation
from the background, camera at eye level, shallow depth with a soft
foreground accent.

BACKGROUND: bold black-to-crimson gradient, blurred generic arena light
flares, no readable signage, no real team or league logos anywhere.

LIGHTING: signature YouTube thumbnail lighting rig — strong key light
sculpting the face, soft dream-light fill, a defined gold-and-crimson back

of four text values).

## 4. Long-form script (full)

> What if you didn't have to choose between a lockdown shooter and a
> rim-running big — and instead ran a point guard who slashes downhill,
> finishes over contact, sets up your teammates, AND punishes a sag with
> catch-and-shoot threes? That's this build. The 6'6 Slashing Playmaker.
> Let's get into it.
>
> **Position & Frame** — Point guard, 6'6", 195 lbs, 6'9" wingspan. Tall
> enough to see over smaller defenders and finish above them; light enough
> to stay quick attacking downhill.
>
> **Attributes** — Playmaking 90 (secondary playmaker, creates off the
> dribble), Finishing 88 (converts through contact), Shooting 84
> (punishes a sag with catch-and-shoot), Physicals 85 (fuels the downhill
> game), Defense 68 / Rebounding 55 (the tradeoffs — offense-first build).
>
> **Badges** — Aerial Wizard (HOF) and Downhill (HOF) carry the finishing
> identity; Deadeye (Gold) protects the catch-and-shoot; Quick First Step
> (Gold) creates the initial separation.
>
> **Takeovers** — Shot Creator (off-the-dribble scoring) and Slasher
> (downhill attacking) — one takeover for whichever the defense gives up.
>
> **The loop:** get downhill, finish or create, punish the sag from three.
>
> **Who it's for** — Competitive Park/Rec players who want one versatile
> day-one build, not a hyper-specialized shooter or a paint-locked big.
>
> **Look** — Black and crimson, gold trim, #4, short fade with a
> line-up, light beard.
>
> **CTA** — Build it, run it in Park/Rec, comment how it feels, like and
> subscribe for the next build.

# Thumbnail Template v2 — "Build Card" (reusable brand system)

**PREVIEW ONLY — nothing rendered.** 3 creative directions below, one pick,
ready for your approval before anything goes to Higgsfield.

## The fix, stated plainly

Your brief asked for LeBron/Jordan/Shai comps + a "99 OVR" badge. That's
real-player-likeness + copied-trade-dress risk, which conflicts with your
own standing rule from earlier this session. Swapped in below: the build's
own real attribute numbers as "Elite Tier" callouts instead of named
players, and an original grade badge instead of 2K's OVR format. Same
flex, same visual energy, zero borrowed risk.

## Reusable template shape (this is the part that scales)

Every future build reuses this exact structure — only 3 things change:
build name, the 3 attribute callouts (pulled straight from that build's
real spec numbers), and the grade badge value. Colors, layout, and type
treatment stay identical every time, which is what makes it recognizable
as "yours" on the scroll.

| Slot | Content | Varies per build? |
|---|---|---|
| Center | Photo-real custom player, cinematic lighting, ~50-55% of frame | Yes — player pose/look |
| Left panel | Dark glass "build card" | No — fixed design |
| Panel title | Build's nickname (e.g. "FOUR-WAY DEMIGOD") | Yes — per build |
| 3 callouts | Build's own top 3 real attributes, labeled "ELITE TIER" | Yes — pulled from spec |
| Badge | Original glowing grade badge (not "OVR") | Yes — value only, never the format |
| Type | Bold gold + white, high-contrast, mobile-legible | No — fixed |
| Background | Clean black-to-crimson gradient | No — fixed |

## Three creative directions

**Variant A — Glass Stat Card**
> Bold, punchy YouTube-thumbnail composite, 16:9, single unified frame. CENTER SUBJECT: [character baseline — 6'6" muscular point guard, medium-brown skin, short fade with crisp line-up, light beard, plain unbranded black jersey with crimson side panels and gold trim, gold number 4, no other marks] in a confident hero pose, cinematic three-point lighting, filling ~50% of frame centered-right, sharp facial focus. LEFT PANEL: a dark frosted-glass card occupying the left third, bold white title "FOUR-WAY DEMIGOD" at the top in a condensed sans-serif with a gold outline, below it three horizontal stat rows each reading a stat name and "ELITE TIER" in gold on a thin dark bar (90 PLAYMAKING, 88 FINISHING, 84 SHOOTING), a glowing gold circular badge in the corner of the card reading "S-TIER" with a soft radial glow. BACKGROUND: black-to-crimson gradient, blurred generic arena light flares, no readable signage. No real logos, no league marks, no text besides what's specified.

*Expected result:* Clean, editorial "stat card meets hero shot" — reads instantly at mobile scale because the card is a single solid shape, not scattered elements.
*Why it could perform:* Highest information-gap efficiency (title + 3 numbers + grade, nothing wasted) and the cleanest to reuse verbatim for every future build — just swap 4 text values.

**Variant B — Vertical Ticker**
> [same center subject] LEFT PANEL: three stacked badge chips running vertically down the left edge, each a small icon (lightning bolt / flame / shield) beside a stat callout, build nickname as a large diagonal-tilted title crossing behind them.

*Expected result:* More dynamic/kinetic feel, less "clean card" and more "sports broadcast lower-third."
*Why it's weaker:* Diagonal title text is harder to keep legible at mobile scale and harder to template consistently build-to-build (tilt angle has to be manually matched every time).

**Variant C — Split Diagonal Banner**
> [same center subject] LEFT PANEL: a bold diagonal color-block banner with the build nickname in huge type, comparison stats as small chips beneath the banner, no card border.

*Expected result:* Highest visual boldness, most "MrBeast-style" aggressive graphic treatment.
*Why it's weaker:* Diagonal color-blocking is the hardest of the three to keep pixel-consistent across renders — the exact template-reuse goal you named is what this variant risks most.

## Pick: Variant A — Glass Stat Card

Cleanest mobile readability, most information-dense without feeling
cluttered, and the easiest of the three to actually template (fixed card
shape, only text values change) — which is the whole point of "same
colors, same layout, just swap the name and comps."
